How this site works

Minimum rating count

A movie appears in top-rated lists and popularity recommendations only if it has at least {{ MIN_RATING_COUNT }} ratings (configurable with MIN_RATING_COUNT). This stops a single 5-star rating from outranking a movie that many people rated.

Privacy

Pages show only averages and counts. You can see your own ratings after logging in; nobody can see another user's ratings. Accounts use a display name and a password hash. No email or real name is stored.

How recommendations are made

  • Popularity-based: the highest average rating among movies that meet the minimum count, excluding movies you already rated.
  • Similarity-based: two movies are similar when people who rated both tend to score them the same way relative to their own average (adjusted cosine similarity). Your suggestions are movies similar to ones you rated, and each result names the movie of yours it was based on. You need at least {{ MIN_USER_RATINGS }} of your own ratings; until then you see popular movies.

Suggestions are educational and based only on the ratings available here. They are not a guarantee of what you will enjoy.

Data

The starter database contains synthetic sample users and ratings generated for demonstration.
